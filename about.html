The idea behind OrbitFood

A neighbourhood table

OrbitFood started with a simple idea: make seasonal cooking feel generous, relaxed, and worth sharing.

Our kitchen follows the local market and our dining room follows the rhythm of the people in it. A quick lunch, a long dinner, or something to take home — there is room for all of it.
